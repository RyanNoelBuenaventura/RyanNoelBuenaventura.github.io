About

My goal is to help people with my work and creations. I have made progress toward this goal by tutoring STEM at the youth and college level, founding and leading my community college's Computer Science Club, working numerous service jobs, studying emergency medicine and earning my EMT certification, and earning my B.S. in Computer Science with the intent of working on ethical and people serving projects. I pride myself on being highly adaptable, from working in a warehouse with my hands to working on a computer solving problems.
